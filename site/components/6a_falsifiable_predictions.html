6. Falsifiable Predictions

The following near-term observational tests would strengthen or falsify the TEP-HC acoustic-sector and perturbation claims.

  1. Small-scale CMB polarization from active scalar perturbations. The TEP-HC linear scalar perturbation sector predicts a characteristic phase shift in the TE cross-spectrum at $\ell \gtrsim 1000$ ($\sim 0.1\%$–$0.3\%$) due to the $\alpha_B \neq 0$ running of the conformal scalar. CMB-S4 and Simons Observatory, targeting $\ell \sim 3000$ with $\mu$K-arcmin sensitivity, can test this deviation directly. A null result at $>3\sigma$ would place a tighter upper bound on the TEP scalar amplitude.
  2. B-mode polarization from native tensor modes. The TEP-TH assigned tensor-pump benchmark yields $r(k_{\rm pivot}) = 1.9 \times 10^{-9}$ and $r_{\rm max} = 5.2 \times 10^{-7}$ at the fiducial profile ($p=0.1$, $n=4$, $\eta_0=14{,}000$ Mpc), with one-parameter variations spanning $r_{\rm max} = 1.4 \times 10^{-5}$ on the curvature-regular slice ($0 \lt p \le \tfrac12$) to $6.1 \times 10^{-5}$ ($0 \lt p \le 1$) — all far below current BICEP/Keck bounds ($r < 0.036$). As established in TEP-TH Section 10.6, the one-parameter scan does not bound simultaneous multi-parameter profile variations ($p=0.5$, $n=2$, $\eta_0=3000$ Mpc reaches a diagnostic maximum near $1.14\times10^{-4}$), and the assigned pump must first be matched to the shared action's tensor normalization before diagnostic values become model-wide B-mode exclusion thresholds. CMB-S4's $r \sim 10^{-4}$ sensitivity will therefore constrain the joint transition-profile domain $(p, n, \eta_0)$ rather than test a single universal cutoff, while a null result remains fully compatible with the curvature-regular temporal-horizon branch.
  3. Acoustic-peak consistency under tep_mode background. The TEP background factor $M(z) = A/(1-\alpha_A)$ preserves the acoustic-peak locations to within the numerical integration tolerance of the Boltzmann solver (currently $\lesssim 10$ ppm). The current hi_class implementation reports $r_s^{\rm TEP}/r_s^{\Lambda\rm CDM} = 0.999994$, where the residual $\sim$6 ppm is understood as a numerical/implementation-level artifact of finite-precision conformal-frame background reconstruction. Any claimed physical deviation from the exact conformal isomorphism must be shown to exceed the standard solver interpolation noise ($\sim 10$ ppm) before it can be interpreted as a falsification of the background mapping, rather than as a C++ integration artifact.
  4. Growth-factor amplitude from native EFT. The TEP-HC active-perturbation closure, now implemented in the regular $\delta\phi$ variable with the corrected kinetic-coefficient map $\alpha_K = w_c\,\alpha_A^2$ (Appendix A.5a), yields $\sigma_8 = 0.824$ at the fiducial parameters ($\epsilon_T = 0.0066$, $z_T = 5$, $n_T = 2$) on the production screened branch ($w_c = 700$) — a $+0.1\%$ enhancement relative to $\Lambda$CDM ($\sigma_8 = 0.823$) at the same cosmology. The registered response scan (results/closure_response_scan.json) makes the prediction sharp and branch-dependent: $w_c \lesssim 0$ is linearly unstable (the canonical image $w_c=-5$ runs away); the minimally stable unscreened branch $w_c = +7$ is excluded ($\sigma_8 \simeq 1.6$); $w_c \gtrsim 7\times10^{2}$ recovers $\Lambda$CDM; and $w_c \sim 6\times10^{3}$ gives $\sigma_8 \simeq 0.76$, the weak-lensing central value, at the price of a $\sim 50\%$ low-$\ell$ ISW excess (directly measured on the corrected build: $+41\%$ at $\ell = 2$, peaking at $+57\%$ within $\ell \le 10$; results/step_24_lowell_isw_scan.json). The screening parameter is not free-floating: it is the effective cosmological kineticity $Z_E = P_{,X}+2XP_{,XX}$ required by, and physically motivated by, the coarse-grained nonlinear $P(X)$ sector (while the exact nonlinear principal-operator matching remains a separate closure; Appendix A.5a), so a measurement of the growth amplitude directly constrains the effective kineticity of the conformal scalar. The Step 23 provenance audit (results/step_23_s8_provenance_audit.json) further establishes that the MCMC posterior mean $\sigma_8 = 0.858 \pm 0.016$ is prior-boundary dominated ($A_{\rm planck}$ and $n_s$ saturate their low-$\ell$ prior ceilings; $\sigma_8$ tracks $A_{\rm planck}$ with correlation $+0.31$). An earlier claim that the active-perturbation $P(k)$ is bitwise identical to the background-only output is retracted — it was produced while the SMG sector was silently disabled; on the screened production branch the genuine active-scalar deviation is $\lesssim 2\%$ in $P(k)$. As Stage-IV weak-lensing surveys (Euclid, Rubin/LSST) resolve their internal calibration systematics, the closure is falsifiable in sign, amplitude, and branch.

Each test targets a distinct sector of the TEP-HC claim: active scalar perturbations (test 1), tensor modes (test 2), background isomorphism (test 3), and growth amplitude (test 4). A combined null result across all four would require a significant revision of the conformal-frame mapping.
